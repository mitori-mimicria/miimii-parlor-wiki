A strategy wiki for MiiMii Parlor, a VRChat world. Machine specs, effect reliability tables and how the parlor works.

* Numbers can change when the parlor adjusts the machines.

Pachinko terms used here

TermMeaning
KakuhenProbability-up mode. Jackpot odds go up until the next jackpot
JitanTime-short mode. Faster spins and electric support, normal odds. Called Chance Time on STAR PARADE
STKakuhen that lasts a set number of spins (10 on RABBIT HOLE)
ReachTwo reels match and the third is still spinning. Super reach is the long, flashy kind
HoldA saved spin (reserve). Hold lamps show them, and their color hints at the result
NoticeYokoku. An effect that hints at the result before or during a reach
Gekiatsu"Super hot." Used here for effects with 50% or more reliability
PremiumA rare effect that guarantees a jackpot
RushThe ST or kakuhen run after a jackpot (Rabbit RUSH on RABBIT HOLE)

For first-timers

  • You don't sit down to take a machine. Press 打つ (Shoot), 玉を借りる (Borrow balls) or 貯玉から出す (Use saved balls) on a free machine (Taking a machine)
  • Shot strength is automatic. STAR PARADE switches to right-shooting by itself during jackpots, kakuhen and jitan
  • Out of Mi? Work as a coffee seller. 3 cups = 250 saved balls (Making money)
  • For some reason, the mysterious antique dealer (Hoshino Shokai) outside the parlor buys your prizes (The mysterious antique dealer)
  • Occult works. Light a cigarette and wait 2 minutes, for example (Occult)
