FAQ

I can't sit in the chair
Sitting is off by default. Press 打つ (Shoot), 玉を借りる (Borrow balls) or 貯玉から出す (Use saved balls) on a free machine to take it. If you want to sit, turn on the 椅子に座る (Sit on chairs) sign at the entrance.
How do I change the strength or switch to right-shooting?
Both are automatic. STAR PARADE and CRYSTAL switch to right-shooting by themselves during jackpots, kakuhen (RUSH) and jitan. RABBIT HOLE is left-shooting all the way.
I want to step away for a bit
Walk 2.2 m away from the machine's seat and it stops shooting. The machine stays kept as long as balls, holds, a jackpot, kakuhen, jitan or the like are left, and frees up 5 minutes after they're gone. Nobody else can take it while it's kept (When you leave your seat).
I ran out of Mi
Deliver 3 cups as a coffee seller for 250 saved balls. Trash pickup gives 10 balls each. You can play with saved balls (Making money).
Do my balls and Mi carry over to my next visit?
Your Mi, saved balls, prizes and your cigarette and drink stock carry over. Balls left at a machine you didn't count go to your saved balls next time.
Where do I exchange prizes for Mi?
The mysterious antique dealer outside, Hoshino Shokai. The little window of the shack left of the spawn point (The mysterious antique dealer).
Can my member rank go down?
No. It's based on the most Mi you've ever held (Member ranks).
Does the occult really work?
Yes. Normal mode on your own machine only, up to x1.3 combined (Occult).
